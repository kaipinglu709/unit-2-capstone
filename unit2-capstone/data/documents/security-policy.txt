Security Policy

All employees must use two-factor authentication. Passwords must be 12 characters minimum and changed every 90 days. Remote access requires VPN at all times. Employees must not share passwords. Data is classified as public, internal, confidential, or restricted. Confidential and restricted data must be encrypted. Personal customer data is restricted and subject to GDPR and CCPA. Security incidents must be reported within 2 hours. Post-incident review completed within 5 business days. All devices must have endpoint protection installed.
