Employee Handbook

Standard working hours are 9am to 5pm Monday to Friday. Flexible working available with manager approval. Core hours are 10am to 3pm. Employees receive 25 days annual leave plus public holidays. Leave requests required 2 weeks in advance. Unused leave carried over up to 5 days. Performance reviews twice per year rated 1 to 5. Quarterly employee satisfaction surveys conducted. Results shared within 30 days. Departments are Engineering, Sales, Customer Success, and Operations.
